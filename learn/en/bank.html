How to open a bank account in the UK: what to say at the bank, in English

You've just moved to the UK. Open a current account and find out when your card will come.

What you need to do

  1. Explain what you want
  2. Say which documents you have
  3. Ask about timing

The conversation

They say

Hi there, what can I do for you today?

Tip: Use 'I'd like to open a current account'.

  • Best reply
    I'd like to open a current account, please. I've just moved here.
    Clear, and the extra detail helps.
  • Understood, but not natural
    Open account.
    Understood, but 'I'd like to open a current account' sounds much better.
  • Doesn't answer
    I'd like to borrow a current account.
    You open an account. You borrow money.
They say

Of course. Have you got photo ID and proof of address?

Tip: Name your documents and check they are enough.

  • Best reply
    I've got my passport and my tenancy agreement. Is that enough?
    Great: you named both and checked.
  • Understood, but not natural
    Passport, yes.
    Good start. Also say what proof of address you have.
  • Doesn't answer
    My address is proof.
    She needs a document that shows your address, like a tenancy agreement or a bill.
They say

That should be fine. I'll just check a few details.

Tip: Ask 'How long will it take for …?'

  • Best reply
    How long will it take for my card to arrive?
    A natural B1 question.
  • Understood, but not natural
    When is my card coming?
    Works fine. "How long will it take …?" is a useful pattern too.
  • Doesn't answer
    How long is the bank open?
    That's about opening hours. Ask about your card.

How it ends

It usually takes about a week. We'll post it to your address.
