How to send a parcel at a UK Post Office: phrases in British English

Send a parcel to Manchester, choose a service and ask for proof of postage.

What you need to do

  1. Say what you're sending
  2. Choose a service
  3. Ask for proof

The conversation

They say

Next, please! What are you sending today?

Tip: Say 'I'd like to send this … to …'

  • Best reply
    Hi, I'd like to send this parcel to Manchester, please.
    Clear and polite.
  • Understood, but not natural
    Parcel. Manchester.
    Understood, but a full sentence is friendlier.
  • Doesn't answer
    I'd like to buy a parcel.
    You send a parcel. You can buy stamps or a box.
They say

First or second class?

Tip: First class is faster; second class is cheaper. Pick one.

  • Best reply
    Second class is fine, thanks.
    Good choice if it is not urgent.
  • Understood, but not natural
    The cheap one.
    It works! The word for it is 'second class'.
  • Doesn't answer
    Yes, please.
    It's a choice question. Say first or second.
They say

Do you want it signed for?

Tip: Say yes and ask for a 'receipt'.

  • Best reply
    Yes, please. Can I have a receipt as well?
    Great: keep the receipt as proof of postage.
  • Understood, but not natural
    Yes, and a paper, please.
    She'll get it, but the word is 'receipt'.
  • Doesn't answer
    No, I'll sign it now.
    'Signed for' means the person who receives it signs.
