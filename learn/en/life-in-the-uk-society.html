Life in the UK test practice: Modern society (free questions with answers)

This is free practice made by New Life UK. It is not the official test or handbook and does not guarantee a pass. Study the official handbook and book the test only on GOV.UK.

The test has 24 questions, you have 45 minutes, and you need 75% to pass (at least 18 correct).

Each country has its own capital: London for England, Edinburgh for Scotland, Cardiff for Wales and Belfast for Northern Ireland. Ben Nevis in Scotland is the highest mountain in the UK.

The year has many traditions. Bonfire Night on the fifth of November remembers the Gunpowder Plot of 1605. On Remembrance Day, the eleventh of November, people wear poppies for those who died in wars. In Scotland, New Year's Eve is called Hogmanay.

Sport matters to many people. Wimbledon is the oldest tennis tournament in the world, the Grand National is a famous horse race at Aintree, and St Andrews in Scotland is known as the home of golf.

Everyday rules matter too. You can start learning to drive a car at seventeen. You need a TV licence to watch live television or BBC iPlayer. In an emergency, call nine nine nine or one one two.

Key facts

  • Edinburgh: Capital of Scotland
  • Cardiff: Capital of Wales
  • Belfast: Capital of Northern Ireland
  • Ben Nevis: Highest mountain in the UK, in Scotland
  • Hogmanay: New Year's Eve in Scotland
  • Bonfire Night: 5 November: remembers the Gunpowder Plot of 1605
  • Remembrance Day: 11 November: poppies for those who died in wars
  • Wimbledon: The oldest tennis tournament in the world
  • Big Ben: The nickname of the great bell in the Elizabeth Tower
  • Eisteddfod: A Welsh festival of music, poetry and dance

Practice questions

What is the capital city of Wales?

  • Cardiff
  • Swansea
  • Belfast
  • Edinburgh
Answer

Cardiff. Cardiff is the capital of Wales.

What is the capital city of Northern Ireland?

  • Belfast
  • Dublin
  • Cardiff
  • Derry
Answer

Belfast. Belfast is the capital of Northern Ireland. Dublin is the capital of the Republic of Ireland, a different country.

What is the capital city of Scotland?

  • Edinburgh
  • Glasgow
  • Aberdeen
  • Dundee
Answer

Edinburgh. Edinburgh is Scotland's capital; Glasgow is its largest city.

What is the highest mountain in the UK?

  • Ben Nevis
  • Snowdon
  • Scafell Pike
  • Slieve Donard
Answer

Ben Nevis. Ben Nevis, in the Scottish Highlands, is the highest mountain in the UK.

What do people in Scotland call New Year's Eve?

  • Hogmanay
  • Burns Night
  • Eisteddfod
  • Boxing Day
Answer

Hogmanay. Hogmanay is the Scottish name for New Year's Eve, 31 December.

Which event does Bonfire Night on 5 November remember?

  • The Gunpowder Plot of 1605
  • The end of the First World War
  • The Battle of Hastings
  • The signing of Magna Carta
Answer

The Gunpowder Plot of 1605. In 1605 a group tried to blow up the Houses of Parliament; the plot failed.

On Remembrance Day, what flowers do many people wear?

  • Poppies
  • Roses
  • Daffodils
  • Thistles
Answer

Poppies. Poppies remember people who died in the First World War and later conflicts.

When is Remembrance Day?

  • 11 November
  • 5 November
  • 25 January
  • 1 March
Answer

11 November. Remembrance Day is 11 November, the day the First World War ended in 1918.

Which sport is played at Wimbledon?

  • Tennis
  • Golf
  • Cricket
  • Rugby
Answer

Tennis. Wimbledon is the oldest tennis tournament in the world.

The Grand National is a famous...

  • Horse race
  • Football cup final
  • Golf championship
  • Rowing race
Answer

Horse race. The Grand National is a horse race held at Aintree, near Liverpool.

Which Scottish town is known as the home of golf?

  • St Andrews
  • Inverness
  • Stirling
  • Perth
Answer

St Andrews. St Andrews is known around the world as the home of golf.

Big Ben is the name of the great bell in the clock tower of the Houses of Parliament. True or false?

  • TRUE
  • FALSE
Answer

TRUE. Big Ben is the nickname of the Great Bell; since 2012 the tower has been called the Elizabeth Tower.

When is Burns Night, which celebrates the Scottish poet Robert Burns?

  • 25 January
  • 30 November
  • 31 December
  • 17 March
Answer

25 January. Burns Night is 25 January, Robert Burns's birthday.

What is an Eisteddfod?

  • A Welsh festival of music, poetry and dance
  • A Scottish New Year party
  • A type of Irish dance
  • A horse race in England
Answer

A Welsh festival of music, poetry and dance. Eisteddfodau celebrate Welsh culture, language, music and poetry.

At what age can you start learning to drive a car in the UK?

  • 17
  • 15
  • 16
  • 21
Answer

17. You can apply for a provisional licence at 15 years and 9 months and usually start driving a car at 17.

What do you need to watch live TV or BBC iPlayer in the UK?

  • A TV licence
  • A driving licence
  • A passport
  • A library card
Answer

A TV licence. A TV licence is needed to watch or record live TV on any channel, or to use BBC iPlayer.

Which number should you call in an emergency?

  • 999 or 112
  • 101
  • 111
  • 911
Answer

999 or 112. 999 and 112 reach police, fire and ambulance. 101 is for non-emergency police calls, and 111 for non-emergency health advice.

Cars must usually have their first MOT test when they are three years old. True or false?

  • TRUE
  • FALSE
Answer

TRUE. In Great Britain the first MOT is due by the third anniversary of registration, then every year.

Who wrote Oliver Twist?

  • Charles Dickens
  • William Shakespeare
  • Thomas Hardy
  • Robert Burns
Answer

Charles Dickens. Charles Dickens wrote Oliver Twist and A Christmas Carol.

What is the national day of Ireland, also celebrated in Northern Ireland, on 17 March?

  • St Patrick's Day
  • St David's Day
  • St George's Day
  • St Andrew's Day
Answer

St Patrick's Day. St Patrick's Day is 17 March and is a bank holiday in Northern Ireland.
