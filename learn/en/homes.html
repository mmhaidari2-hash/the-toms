How to rent a flat in the UK: talking to a letting agent in British English

You're at Hartley & Wren Homes after a viewing. Find out about bills, the deposit and the Right to Rent check.

What you need to do

  1. Ask what the rent includes
  2. Check the deposit is protected
  3. Ask what you need for the Right to Rent check

The conversation

They say

So, what did you think of the flat?

Tip: Say you liked it, then ask 'Are any bills included?'

  • Best reply
    I really liked it. Are any bills included in the rent?
    Polite and precise: exactly what to ask.
  • Understood, but not natural
    Nice. Bills?
    She understands, but a full question sounds better: 'Are any bills included?'
  • Doesn't answer
    Can I pay more than the asking rent to get it?
    Since May 2026 agents in England can't accept offers above the advertised rent.
They say

No bills, I'm afraid. The deposit is five weeks' rent.

Tip: Ask if it goes into a protection scheme.

  • Best reply
    Will the deposit be protected in a government-approved scheme?
    Great: landlords must protect it within 30 days.
  • Understood, but not natural
    Is the deposit safe?
    Good idea. The key word is 'protected': 'Is it in a protection scheme?'
  • Doesn't answer
    Can I pay you the deposit in cash with no receipt?
    Always get a receipt, and make sure the deposit is protected.
They say

Of course, it'll be with the DPS. Last thing: we need to do your Right to Rent check.

Tip: Ask what you need: 'What do I need to show you?'

  • Best reply
    What do I need to show you? I can send a share code from GOV.UK.
    Perfect: a share code is the online way to prove it.
  • Understood, but not natural
    What do you need?
    Fine. You could also offer: 'I can send a share code.'
  • Doesn't answer
    Why me? Do you only check foreigners?
    Agents must check every adult tenant, British or not.

How it ends

Brilliant. Send me the code tonight and I'll get the paperwork started.
