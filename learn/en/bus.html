How to catch a bus in the UK: what to say to the driver, in British English

You need the 38 bus to the hospital. Check the route, pay and ask where to get off.

What you need to do

  1. Check the bus goes there
  2. Pay the right way
  3. Ask where to get off

The conversation

They say

Morning.

Tip: Ask 'Does this bus go to …?'

  • Best reply
    Morning. Does this bus go to the hospital?
    Perfect question.
  • Understood, but not natural
    Hospital?
    He'll understand, but a full question is more polite.
  • Doesn't answer
    Two tickets to the hospital, please.
    London buses don't sell tickets on board: you tap a card. First check the route.
They say

Yeah, it does. Just tap on the reader.

Tip: Ask if your bank card works.

  • Best reply
    Can I use my contactless bank card?
    Yes: contactless cards, phones and travel cards all work.
  • Understood, but not natural
    Card here?
    Understood, but a full question sounds better.
  • Doesn't answer
    Can I pay with cash?
    London buses don't take cash. Use a contactless card, a travel card or your phone.
They say

Yep, that's fine. Take a seat.

Tip: Ask politely: 'Could you tell me when …?'

  • Best reply
    Could you tell me when we get to the hospital, please?
    Lovely and polite.
  • Understood, but not natural
    Tell me the hospital stop.
    It sounds like an order. 'Could you …, please?' is politer.
  • Doesn't answer
    Where is the hospital doctor?
    The driver can tell you the stop, not the doctor.

How it ends

No problem. It's about ten minutes. I'll give you a shout.
