# Short-video ideas — New Life UK

Ten vertical videos for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Each is 30–45 seconds. Record on a real phone from the current app (or the store screenshots in `store/` as a last resort for storyboards only). Do not publish until the owner approves and `[PLAY_LINK]` is ready.

**On-screen rules:** keep captions short, English first, optional Persian line at the end. Say “practice,” never “official test” or “guaranteed pass.” Do not say the app works fully offline, is a general AI, or can get someone a visa. End card: *New Life UK — everyday British English* plus `[PLAY_LINK]` and “18+”.

Record in a quiet room, portrait, with the system nav bar clean if you can. A cheap clip-on mic is enough. One idea per post; do not stitch all ten into a single launch dump.

---

### 1. Five sentences you need on day one

- **Hook:** “Five British-English lines I wish I had on my first day in the UK.”
- **Screen to record:** Map home (`/`), then a shop conversation (`/chat/l1_u43_1` or High Street shop).
- **Script (about 40s):** “Excuse me, can you help me? / That’s all, thank you. / Is this the right bus for the High Street? / I have an appointment. / Could I have a look at this flat?” Show the shop chat as you say the first line. “New Life UK lets you practise these in everyday situations before you need them for real.”
- **On-screen text:** *5 lines for day one* → each sentence as a caption → *Practise before you need it* → *New Life UK · 18+*.

### 2. Swap roles at the shop

- **Hook:** “Don’t just learn the customer’s line. Practise the shop assistant too.”
- **Screen to record:** Shop conversation with the role-swap control visible (`/chat/l1_u43_1`).
- **Script:** “Most apps only let you be the learner. In New Life UK you can swap roles and answer as the assistant: ‘Of course! What are you looking for?’ That is how a real conversation feels.”
- **On-screen text:** *Play both sides* → *Learner ↔ assistant* → *Everyday British English*.

### 3. The nursery interview

- **Hook:** “Nervous about a UK nursery interview? Practise the English, not the job.”
- **Screen to record:** Nursery pack (`/pack/nursery`) then the interview lesson or chat (`/chat/nur_0_1` or `/lesson/nur_0_1`).
- **Script:** “This pack is English-language practice for early-years work: the interview, first day, parents, logs. It is not professional training and it does not replace your employer’s induction. But you can hear the phrases and say them out loud before the real meeting.”
- **On-screen text:** *Nursery interview English* → *Practice only, not a qualification* → *New Life UK*.

### 4. Walk into the library

- **Hook:** “The library in this app is a room you walk through, not a file list.”
- **Screen to record:** Library facade, hall, then shelves (`/library`, enter, walk forward, open shelves).
- **Script:** “You come in off the street, walk the hall, and open a bookcase. A1 and A2 shelves stay easy to reach. Open a book, look up a word, keep going. Relaxed practice, not a test.”
- **On-screen text:** *Walk in* → *Open the shelves* → *Read at your level*.

### 5. At the GP

- **Hook:** “What do you actually say at a UK GP reception?”
- **Screen to record:** A health conversation (for example `/chat/okafor_gp`) or the forms practice that links out to NHS pages. Keep any disclaimer on screen for a beat.
- **Script:** “I’d like to register, please. / It’s a regular prescription. / Is there a cancellation today? You can practise these conversations in the app. For official health information, use the NHS website — the app is not an NHS service.”
- **On-screen text:** *English for the GP* → *Independent app, not the NHS* → *Always check official pages*.

### 6. Life in the UK — practice, honestly

- **Hook:** “Want to practise Life in the UK questions? Here’s what this app is — and isn’t.”
- **Screen to record:** Life in the UK hub (`/lifeuk`), one chapter or a short practice question.
- **Script:** “New Life UK has practice questions so you can learn the vocabulary and ideas. These are not the official test and they do not guarantee a pass. When you are ready for the real thing, use the official materials.”
- **On-screen text:** *Practice questions* → *Not the official test* → *No guaranteed pass*.

### 7. Listen, then say it

- **Hook:** “Listen to the line. Then say it yourself.”
- **Screen to record:** Any conversation with the audio / repeat control visible (shop, breakfast, or nursery). Do not claim every line has bundled audio.
- **Script:** “Tap to hear available British-English model audio. Repeat it. Then choose your reply. Some clips come with the app; others download when you need them. Use it on the bus, then try the same line in real life.”
- **On-screen text:** *Listen* → *Repeat* → *Reply* → *British English*.

### 8. Help in your own language

- **Hook:** “The help is in your language. The English stays British.”
- **Screen to record:** A bilingual lesson or helper-language setting (Persian on screen is ideal; Arabic, Hindi, Pashto or Urdu also work).
- **Script:** “If you tap a line, you can get help in Persian — or Arabic, Hindi, Pashto or Urdu. The English you practise is still British English. That is the point.”
- **On-screen text:** *Helper language on* → *English stays British* → *AR · HI · PS · FA · UR*.

### 9. The town map

- **Hook:** “This is not a menu. It’s a neighbourhood.”
- **Screen to record:** Full-screen town map (`/`). Pinch to zoom in (the map does not shrink below the screen). Tap a place such as Home, the High Street or the library.
- **Script:** “Open the app and you’re on a UK street. Zoom in, pick a place, and start a lesson set in that spot — the house, the shop, the library. A simple way to see what to practise today.”
- **On-screen text:** *A UK neighbourhood* → *Tap a place to practise* → *New Life UK*.

### 10. Founder to camera, then the app

- **Hook:** “I built this because I needed it.”
- **Screen to record:** You speaking for 15–20 seconds, then a cut to the map and one conversation. End on the 3D house icon and the Play link.
- **Script:** “I’m [YOUR_NAME]. I came to the UK and I wanted everyday English, not another generic course. So I built New Life UK for adults: real situations, British English, help in your language. It’s independent — not a government app, not legal advice. If it helps you, the link is on the screen.”
- **On-screen text:** *A learning app for adults 18+* → *Independent, not official advice* → `[PLAY_LINK]`.

## Filming notes

- Prefer real-device capture over emulator. If a notification banner appears, recut.
- Do not film premium lock overlays as if content is blocked in the current beta (`betaFree` is on).
- Background music optional and quiet; avoid copyrighted tracks.
- First three to film if time is short: videos 1, 3 and 10.
