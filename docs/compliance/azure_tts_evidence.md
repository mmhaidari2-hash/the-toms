Recorded on 5 November 2026. No keys, card numbers or account IDs are stored here.

## Who generated the voices
- Service: Microsoft Azure AI Speech, prebuilt neural voices (for example en-GB-OliviaNeural for Ms Ellis), region UK South.
- Account holder: the app owner personally (confirmed by the owner on 5 November 2026). It is not yet a company account.
- Tiers: Standard S1 (paid) until early November 2026. From 5 November 2026, only the Free F0 resource is used, capped by the tool at 480,000 characters a month (`app/tool/audit/tts_month.json`).

## Terms that apply (as read on 5 November 2026)
- [Microsoft Enterprise AI Services Code of Conduct](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/ai-code-of-conduct): customers must "disclose when the output, decisions, or actions are generated by AI, including the synthetic nature of generated voices". They must not use the services "to deceive or intentionally misinform" or "to impersonate any person without explicit and valid consent". The app meets this with the AI-voice note in the menu and the About page (tested in `test/legal_test.dart`). The characters are fictional; no real person's voice is cloned, and no custom or personal voice models are used.
- [Microsoft Azure terms (MCA product offering)](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/terms/en-us/productoffering/microsoftazure/mca): the general Azure licence terms that govern the subscription.
