## Definitions used

- [Play Data safety form](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469): “Collect” means data transmitted off the device, including by a library or SDK, to the developer or to a third party. On-device processing that is not sent off the device is not collected. “Sharing” is transfer of collected data to a third party, including an on-device transfer to another app. A service provider acting on the developer’s instructions, and a transfer the user starts and reasonably expects, are not declared as sharing. Location inferred from an IP address is called out under precise location. Ephemeral processing is still answered in the form, and is disclosed on the store page only if it fails Play’s memory-only test.
- [User Data policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311)
- [What users are told the section means](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/11416267)
- [RevenueCat: Google Play’s Data safety](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/platform-resources/google-platform-resources/google-plays-data-safety) — this page is for an app that **uses** RevenueCat.
- [GitHub Pages: data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages) and [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) (effective date on the page: 27 April 2026).
