## App name

'The Toms' overlaps with the TOMS shoe trademark (different class, but a risk). Not renamed yet. Proposals:

1. **Next Door English** — the neighbours idea, plain and descriptive.
2. **Kettle On** — very British ("I'll put the kettle on"), warm, easy to say.
3. **Hackney Lane English** — fictional street, keeps the setting.

Check each on the UK IPO and EUIPO trademark search and app stores before choosing.
