# b1_pp | B1 | Present perfect: ever, never, just, already, yet | حال کامل: ever، never، just، already، yet | R: Present perfect|present perfect|I've been
T: Use the present perfect for life experience and for past actions that matter now: I've been to Scotland. I've lost my keys! | حال کامل برای تجربه‌های زندگی و کارهای گذشته‌ای است که الان مهم‌اند: I've been to Scotland. I've lost my keys!
T: Make it with have or has + the past participle: I've seen, she's finished, they haven't eaten. | با have/has + اسم مفعول ساخته می‌شود: I've seen، she's finished، they haven't eaten.
T: "Just" means a moment ago, "already" means sooner than expected, and "yet" goes at the end of questions and negatives: Have you finished yet? | just یعنی همین الان، already یعنی زودتر از انتظار، و yet در آخر سؤال و منفی می‌آید: Have you finished yet?
E: Have you ever been to Edinburgh? | تا حالا ادینبرو رفته‌ای؟
E: William has just broken a mug. | ویلیام همین الان یک لیوان را شکست.
E: I haven't done the washing-up yet. | هنوز ظرف‌ها را نشسته‌ام.

# b1_pp_past | B1 | Present perfect or past simple? | حال کامل یا گذشتهٔ ساده؟ | R: Present perfect vs past simple|Present perfect
T: Past simple for a finished time we name: yesterday, last year, in 2015, when I was a child. | گذشتهٔ ساده برای زمانی تمام‌شده که اسمش را می‌آوریم: yesterday، last year، in 2015.
T: Present perfect when the time isn't finished or isn't said: today, this week, ever, recently. | حال کامل وقتی زمان تمام نشده یا گفته نمی‌شود: today، this week، ever، recently.
T: So we say "I've been to Wales" but "I went to Wales last summer". Never "I've been there last summer". | پس می‌گوییم I've been to Wales ولی I went to Wales last summer.

# b1_future | B1 | Future forms: will, going to, present continuous | زمان آینده: will، going to و حال استمراری | R: Future forms|will / going to
T: "Going to" is for plans you've already made: I'm going to paint the kitchen. | going to برای برنامه‌هایی است که از قبل گرفته‌اید: I'm going to paint the kitchen.
T: "Will" is for quick decisions, offers and predictions: It's cold. I'll make some tea. I think it'll rain. | will برای تصمیم لحظه‌ای، پیشنهاد کمک و پیش‌بینی: I'll make some tea. I think it'll rain.
T: The present continuous is for fixed arrangements with a time: We're seeing the dentist on Friday. | حال استمراری برای قرارهای قطعی با زمان مشخص: We're seeing the dentist on Friday.
E: Don't worry, I'll pick you up from the station. | نگران نباش، از ایستگاه دنبالت می‌آیم.
E: Ryan's going to learn to drive next year. | رایان سال بعد قرار است رانندگی یاد بگیرد.
E: We're having a barbecue on Saturday. | شنبه کباب‌پزی داریم.

# b1_first_cond | B1 | First conditional: If it rains, we'll… | شرطی نوع اول: If it rains, we'll… | R: First conditional|If you…, you'll|If you…, I'll
T: Use the first conditional for real possibilities in the future: If it rains, we'll stay in. | شرطی نوع اول برای احتمال واقعی در آینده: If it rains, we'll stay in.
T: The pattern is If + present simple, then will + verb. Don't put "will" after "if": If you hurry, not If you will hurry. | الگو: If + حال ساده، سپس will + فعل. بعد از if از will استفاده نکنید.
T: "Unless" means "if not": We'll be late unless we leave now. | unless یعنی «اگر نه»: We'll be late unless we leave now.
E: If you finish your tea, you can have pudding. | اگر شامت را تمام کنی، می‌توانی دسر بخوری.
E: If Biscuit sees a bird, she'll sit at the window all day. | اگر بیسکویت یک پرنده ببیند، تمام روز پشت پنجره می‌نشیند.
E: I won't go unless you come with me. | نمی‌روم مگر اینکه تو هم با من بیایی.

# b1_modals | B1 | Modals: have to, must, should, might | فعل‌های وجهی: have to، must، should، might | R: have to / don't have to|Modals of possibility|Giving advice
T: "Have to" and "must" mean it's necessary: I have to work on Saturday. You must wear a seat belt. | have to و must یعنی لازم است: I have to work on Saturday.
T: "Don't have to" means it isn't necessary, but "mustn't" means don't do it: You don't have to come. You mustn't smoke here. | don't have to یعنی لازم نیست، ولی mustn't یعنی ممنوع است.
T: "Should" gives advice, and "might" or "could" means maybe: You should see a GP. It might snow tonight. | should برای توصیه، might و could یعنی شاید: You should see a GP. It might snow tonight.
E: You don't have to wear school uniform on Friday. | جمعه لازم نیست لباس مدرسه بپوشی.
E: You mustn't feed Biscuit chocolate. | نباید به بیسکویت شکلات بدهی.
E: Take a coat. It might get chilly later. | کت بردار. شاید بعداً سرد شود.
