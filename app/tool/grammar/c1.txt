# c1_inversion | C1 | Inversion: Never have I… / Not only… | وارونگی: Never have I… / Not only… | R: Formal discourse markers|Formal spoken English|Persuasive presentation
T: After a negative or limiting word at the start, we swap the subject and auxiliary, like a question: Never have I seen such rain. | بعد از یک کلمهٔ منفی یا محدودکننده در ابتدای جمله، فاعل و فعل کمکی جابه‌جا می‌شوند، مثل سؤال: Never have I seen such rain.
T: Common openers: Never, Rarely, Seldom, Not only… but also, Hardly… when, No sooner… than, Under no circumstances, Only then. | شروع‌کننده‌های رایج: Never، Rarely، Seldom، Not only… but also، Hardly… when، No sooner… than، Under no circumstances، Only then.
T: If there's no auxiliary, add do, does or did: Rarely does Tom complain. It sounds formal or dramatic, so save it for speeches, writing and storytelling. | اگر فعل کمکی نیست do/does/did اضافه کنید: Rarely does Tom complain. لحنش رسمی یا نمایشی است؛ برای سخنرانی، نوشتن و قصه‌گویی.
E: Never have I been so proud of this family. | هیچ‌وقت این‌قدر به این خانواده افتخار نکرده بودم.
E: Not only did Ryan pass, but he also got the top mark. | رایان نه‌تنها قبول شد، بلکه بالاترین نمره را هم گرفت.
E: No sooner had we sat down than the fire alarm went off. | تازه نشسته بودیم که آژیر آتش به صدا درآمد.

P: gap | Rarely ___ Leslie leave work before six. | does ; is ; she does

P: choose | Which sentence is correct? | Under no circumstances should you open this door. ; Under no circumstances you should open this door. ; Under no circumstances should open you this door.
P: order | Hardly had the match started when it began to pour.
P: transform | Start with "Never": "I have never felt so tired." | Never have I felt so tired.
M: gap | No sooner ___ we arrived than the rain stopped. | had ; have ; did
M: gap | Seldom ___ you see a fox in the daytime. | do ; you do ; are
M: choose | Which is the formal, inverted version? | Only then did I realise my mistake. ; Only then I realised my mistake. ; Only then I did realise my mistake.
M: order | Not only is she clever, but she is also kind.
M: transform | Start with "Rarely": "We rarely eat out on a weekday." | Rarely do we eat out on a weekday.

# c1_mixed_cond | C1 | Mixed conditionals | شرطی‌های ترکیبی | R: Mixed conditionals|Third conditional
T: Mixed conditionals join two times. A past condition with a present result: If I'd taken that job, I'd be living in Manchester now. | شرطی ترکیبی دو زمان را به هم وصل می‌کند. شرط گذشته با نتیجهٔ حال: If I'd taken that job, I'd be living in Manchester now.
T: Or a present state with a past result: If I weren't so shy, I would have spoken to her. | یا حالت حال با نتیجهٔ گذشته: If I weren't so shy, I would have spoken to her.
T: Check the time of each half: "had + past participle" looks back, "would + verb" is about now, "would have + past participle" is about the past. | زمان هر نیمه را بسنجید: had + اسم مفعول به گذشته نگاه می‌کند، would + فعل دربارهٔ حال است، would have + اسم مفعول دربارهٔ گذشته.
E: If Tom hadn't missed that bus, he wouldn't have met Leslie, and Nicola wouldn't be here! | اگر تام آن اتوبوس را از دست نداده بود، لزلی را نمی‌دید و حالا نیکولا اینجا نبود!
E: If I were better with money, I wouldn't have bought that sofa. | اگر حساب پول دستم بود، آن کاناپه را نمی‌خریدم.
E: If Ryan had revised, he'd be on holiday now instead of resitting. | اگر رایان درس خوانده بود، حالا به‌جای امتحان مجدد در تعطیلات بود.
P: gap | If I had gone to bed earlier, I ___ so tired now. | wouldn't be ; wouldn't have been ; won't be
P: gap | If she ___ afraid of flying, she would have come to Spain with us. | weren't ; hadn't been ; isn't
P: gap | If we'd bought that house, we ___ next door to Gran now. | 'd be living ; 'd have lived ; 'll live
P: choose | Which is a past condition with a present result? | If I'd learnt Welsh as a child, I'd speak it now. ; If I learnt Welsh, I'd speak it. ; If I learn Welsh, I'll speak it.
P: order | If he had saved more, he would be retired now.
P: transform | Make it a mixed conditional: "I didn't eat breakfast, so I'm hungry now." | If I had eaten breakfast, I wouldn't be hungry now. ; If I'd eaten breakfast, I wouldn't be hungry now. ; If I had eaten breakfast, I would not be hungry now.
M: gap | If Leslie ___ the train, she'd be home by now. | had caught ; caught ; would catch
M: gap | If I were taller, I ___ joined the basketball team years ago. | would have ; would ; had
M: choose | Which sentence is correct? | If you hadn't lost the map, we wouldn't be lost now. ; If you didn't lose the map, we wouldn't be lost now. ; If you hadn't lost the map, we won't be lost now.
M: order | If I spoke French, I would have applied.
M: transform | Make it a mixed conditional: "He isn't careful, so he broke the vase." | If he were careful, he wouldn't have broken the vase. ; If he was careful, he wouldn't have broken the vase. ; If he were more careful, he wouldn't have broken the vase.

# c1_wish | C1 | wish, if only and it's time | wish، if only و it's time | R: wish / hope|Third conditional
T: Wish or if only + past simple for present regrets: I wish I lived nearer the sea. If only it weren't raining. | wish یا if only + گذشتهٔ ساده برای حسرت دربارهٔ حال: I wish I lived nearer the sea.
T: Wish + past perfect for past regrets: I wish I hadn't said that. Wish + would for annoying habits: I wish you'd stop tapping! | wish + ماضی بعید برای حسرت گذشته: I wish I hadn't said that. wish + would برای عادت‌های آزاردهنده: I wish you'd stop tapping!
T: "It's time" or "It's high time" + past simple means it should happen now: It's high time we left. | It's (high) time + گذشتهٔ ساده یعنی وقتش رسیده: It's high time we left.
E: I wish I had more time with the kids. | کاش وقت بیشتری با بچه‌ها داشتم.
E: If only we'd booked a table! | کاش میز رزرو کرده بودیم!
E: I wish Biscuit would stop sleeping on my clean washing. | کاش بیسکویت این‌قدر روی لباس‌های تمیزم نخوابد.
P: gap | I wish I ___ drive. The bus takes ages. | could ; can ; will
P: gap | If only I ___ that last slice of cake. I feel awful. | hadn't eaten ; didn't eat ; wouldn't eat
P: gap | It's high time you ___ your room, Ryan. | tidied ; tidy ; will tidy
P: choose | Which is about an annoying habit? | I wish you would put the lid back on. ; I wish you put the lid back on yesterday. ; I wish you had put the lid back on.
P: order | I wish we had gone to the beach.
P: transform | Make a wish about now: "I don't have a garden." | I wish I had a garden. ; If only I had a garden.
M: gap | I wish it ___ so cold today. | weren't ; isn't ; won't be
M: gap | She wishes she ___ the job. She regrets it now. | had taken ; took ; would take
M: choose | Which sentence is correct? | It's time we went home. ; It's time we go home. ; It's time we will go home.
M: order | If only I knew the answer.
M: transform | Make a past regret with "wish": "I didn't bring an umbrella." | I wish I had brought an umbrella. ; I wish I'd brought an umbrella.

# c1_cleft | C1 | Cleft sentences: What I need is… / It was… who… | جملات شکافته: What I need is… / It was… who… | R: Formal spoken English|Expressing strong preferences|Narrative tenses
T: Cleft sentences split one idea into two parts to point at the important bit: What I need is a cup of tea. | جملهٔ شکافته یک ایده را دو تکه می‌کند تا روی بخش مهم تأکید کند: What I need is a cup of tea.
T: "It is / It was … that / who …" puts the focus at the front: It was Nicola who broke the vase, not William. | It is / It was … that / who … تأکید را جلو می‌آورد: It was Nicola who broke the vase, not William.
T: Other useful patterns: All I want is…, The thing is…, The reason why… is…, What happened was… | الگوهای دیگر: All I want is…، The thing is…، The reason why… is…، What happened was…
E: What I love about Hackney is the markets. | چیزی که در هکنی دوست دارم بازارهایش است.
E: It was Tom who found Biscuit in the end. | آخرش تام بود که بیسکویت را پیدا کرد.
E: All I want for my birthday is a quiet day. | تنها چیزی که برای تولدم می‌خواهم یک روز آرام است.

# c1_causative | C1 | Causative and advanced passive: have it done, is said to… | سببی و مجهول پیشرفته: have it done، is said to… | R: have something done|Passive reporting
T: Use have or get + object + past participle when someone else does a job for you: We're having the boiler serviced. I got my hair cut. | have یا get + مفعول + اسم مفعول وقتی کسی کاری را برایتان انجام می‌دهد: We're having the boiler serviced.
T: The same pattern is used for bad things that happen to you: Tom had his bike stolen. | همین الگو برای اتفاق بدی است که برایتان می‌افتد: Tom had his bike stolen.
T: For reports and rumours, use the passive with say, believe or think: It is said that… or He is believed to be… For the past: She is thought to have left. | برای گزارش و شایعه: It is said that… یا He is believed to be… برای گذشته: She is thought to have left.
E: Leslie is getting her eyes tested on Friday. | لزلی جمعه چشم‌هایش را معاینه می‌کند.
E: We had a new kitchen fitted last spring. | بهار گذشته آشپزخانهٔ جدید برایمان نصب کردند.
E: The pub is said to be the oldest in London. | می‌گویند این میخانه قدیمی‌ترین میخانهٔ لندن است.
