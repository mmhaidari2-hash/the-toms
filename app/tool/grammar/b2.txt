# b2_second_cond | B2 | Second conditional: If I won, I would… | شرطی نوع دوم: If I won, I would… | R: Second conditional|If I were you
T: Use the second conditional for imaginary or unlikely situations now or in the future: If I won the lottery, I'd buy a cottage. | شرطی نوع دوم برای موقعیت‌های خیالی یا بعید در حال یا آینده: If I won the lottery, I'd buy a cottage.
T: The pattern is If + past simple, then would + verb. It's about now, even though it uses a past form. | الگو: If + گذشتهٔ ساده، سپس would + فعل. با اینکه شکل گذشته دارد، دربارهٔ حال است.
T: For advice, British people often say "If I were you…": If I were you, I'd ring them. | برای توصیه زیاد می‌گویند If I were you: If I were you, I'd ring them.
E: If I didn't have work, I'd stay in bed all day. | اگر کار نداشتم، تمام روز در رختخواب می‌ماندم.
E: If we had a garden, we'd get a trampoline. | اگر حیاط داشتیم، ترامپولین می‌خریدیم.
E: If I were you, I'd take the train. | من جای تو بودم، با قطار می‌رفتم.

# b2_third_cond | B2 | Third conditional and regrets | شرطی نوع سوم و حسرت | R: Third conditional|should have|Mixed conditionals
T: Use the third conditional to imagine a different past: If I'd left earlier, I wouldn't have missed the train. | شرطی نوع سوم برای تصور گذشته‌ای متفاوت: If I'd left earlier, I wouldn't have missed the train.
T: The pattern is If + had + past participle, then would have + past participle. | الگو: If + had + اسم مفعول، سپس would have + اسم مفعول.
T: For regrets and criticism, use should have or shouldn't have: I should have listened. You shouldn't have shouted. | برای حسرت و انتقاد should have / shouldn't have: I should have listened.
E: If Ryan had revised, he would have passed. | اگر رایان درس خوانده بود، قبول می‌شد.
E: If we hadn't stopped for chips, we'd have been on time. | اگر برای چیپس نایستاده بودیم، سر وقت رسیده بودیم.
E: I should have brought a coat. It's freezing! | باید کت آورده بودم. خیلی سرد است!

# b2_passive | B2 | The passive: It was built in 1890 | مجهول: It was built in 1890 | R: Passive|passive
T: Use the passive when the action matters more than who does it, or when we don't know who: The bins are collected on Tuesdays. | مجهول وقتی کار مهم‌تر از کننده است یا کننده را نمی‌دانیم: The bins are collected on Tuesdays.
T: Make it with be + past participle: it is made, it was taken, it has been sold, it will be delivered. | با be + اسم مفعول: it is made، it was taken، it has been sold، it will be delivered.
T: Add "by" only if the person is important: The bridge was designed by a famous engineer. | فقط اگر کننده مهم است by بیاورید: designed by a famous engineer.
E: Our street was built in the 1890s. | خیابان ما در دههٔ ۱۸۹۰ ساخته شد.
E: Tom's bike has been stolen! | دوچرخهٔ تام دزدیده شده!
E: Your parcel will be delivered tomorrow. | بستهٔ شما فردا تحویل داده می‌شود.

# b2_reported | B2 | Reported speech: She said she was… | نقل قول غیرمستقیم: She said she was… | R: Reported speech|Reported questions|Reporting verbs

# b2_used_to | B2 | used to, would and be used to | used to، would و be used to | R: used to|past routines|Describing past routines
T: "Used to" is for past habits and states that aren't true now: I used to live in Leeds. | used to برای عادت‌ها و حالت‌های گذشته که دیگر درست نیستند: I used to live in Leeds.
T: "Would" can also describe past habits, but not states: Every summer we'd go to Skegness. | would هم برای عادت گذشته است، ولی نه حالت: Every summer we'd go to Skegness.
T: "Be used to" or "get used to" + -ing means something is normal for you now: I'm used to driving on the left. | be/get used to + ing یعنی چیزی برایتان عادی شده: I'm used to driving on the left.
E: Tom used to have long hair. | تام قبلاً موی بلند داشت.
E: On Sundays, Gran would make a huge roast. | یکشنبه‌ها مادربزرگ یک کباب بزرگ درست می‌کرد.
E: I'm still getting used to the British weather. | هنوز دارم به هوای بریتانیا عادت می‌کنم.
