# c1_future_past | C1 | Future perfect continuous and the future in the past | آیندهٔ کامل استمراری و «آینده در گذشته» | R: Narrative tenses
T: The future perfect continuous (will have been + -ing) looks back from a future point at how long something has lasted: By May, I'll have been living here for ten years. | آیندهٔ کامل استمراری (will have been + ing) از نقطه‌ای در آینده به مدت یک کار نگاه می‌کند: By May, I'll have been living here for ten years.
T: The future in the past describes what was planned or expected at an earlier time: was going to, would, was about to, was to. | «آینده در گذشته» آنچه را در گذشته برنامه‌ریزی یا انتظار می‌رفت بیان می‌کند: was going to، would، was about to، was to.
T: "Was going to" often shows a plan that didn't happen: I was going to call you, but I forgot. | was going to اغلب برنامه‌ای را نشان می‌دهد که انجام نشد: I was going to call you, but I forgot.
E: In June, Tom will have been driving buses for twenty years. | در ژوئن، بیست سال است که تام اتوبوس می‌راند.
E: I knew she would be a brilliant nurse. | می‌دانستم پرستار فوق‌العاده‌ای می‌شود.
E: We were about to leave when the phone rang. | داشتیم می‌رفتیم که تلفن زنگ زد.
P: gap | By the time he retires, he'll have been ___ there for forty years. | working ; worked ; work
P: gap | I ___ going to tell you, but you found out first. | was ; am ; will
P: gap | She promised she ___ write every week. | would ; will ; was
P: choose | Which shows a plan that didn't happen? | We were going to buy a house, but prices went up. ; We will buy a house. ; We have bought a house.
P: order | I was about to call you.
P: transform | Use the future perfect continuous: "I started this job in 2020. In 2030…" (In 2030 I'll…) | In 2030 I'll have been doing this job for ten years. ; In 2030 I will have been doing this job for ten years.
M: gap | Little did they know that they ___ never see each other again. | would ; will ; are
M: gap | By midnight we'll have been ___ for twelve hours. | travelling ; travelled ; travel
M: choose | Which is the future in the past? | He said he would be late. ; He says he will be late. ; He is late.
M: order | The meeting was to start at nine.
M: transform | Use "was going to": "I planned to cook, but I was too tired." (I was going to…) | I was going to cook, but I was too tired.

# c1_modals | C1 | Advanced modals: needn't have, be bound to, may well | افعال وجهی پیشرفته: needn't have، be bound to، may well | R: Modals of possibility
T: "Needn't have" + past participle means you did something that wasn't necessary: I needn't have cooked — they'd already eaten. | needn't have + اسم مفعول یعنی کاری کردی که لازم نبود: I needn't have cooked.
T: "Didn't need to" means it wasn't necessary, and usually you didn't do it: I didn't need to queue, so I went straight in. | didn't need to یعنی لازم نبود و معمولاً انجامش ندادی: I didn't need to queue.
T: "Be bound to" means certain; "may/might well" means quite likely; "could hardly" means it was almost impossible. | be bound to یعنی حتماً؛ may/might well یعنی به احتمال زیاد؛ could hardly یعنی تقریباً ناممکن بود.
E: You needn't have brought a present, but thank you! | لازم نبود هدیه بیاوری، ولی ممنون!
E: It's bound to rain on the day of the barbecue. | حتماً روز کباب‌پزی باران می‌آید.
E: The trains may well be delayed because of the strike. | به احتمال زیاد قطارها به خاطر اعتصاب تأخیر دارند.
P: gap | We ___ have rushed. The film started late. | needn't ; didn't need ; mustn't
P: gap | He's always late. He's ___ to miss the start. | bound ; may ; sure that
P: gap | Prices may ___ go up again next year. | well ; good ; much
P: choose | Which means you did something unnecessary? | I needn't have taken a taxi. ; I didn't need to take a taxi. ; I mustn't take a taxi.
P: order | You needn't have waited for me.
P: transform | Use "needn't have": "I bought milk, but there was plenty." (I…) | I needn't have bought milk. ; I need not have bought milk.
M: gap | I could ___ believe my eyes. | hardly ; hard ; nearly
M: gap | Don't worry, she's ___ to call soon. | bound ; may ; might well
M: choose | Which means "quite likely"? | The shop might well be closed. ; The shop can't be closed. ; The shop needn't be closed.
M: order | It's bound to be busy on Saturday.
M: transform | Use "may well": "It's quite likely that they'll win." (They…) | They may well win. ; They might well win.

# c1_subjunctive | C1 | Formal subjunctive: It's essential that he be… | وجه التزامی رسمی: It's essential that he be… | R: Formal
T: After formal verbs and adjectives like insist, recommend, suggest, demand, essential and vital, British English often uses "should" + verb or the bare verb. | بعد از فعل‌ها و صفت‌های رسمی مثل insist، recommend، suggest، demand، essential و vital، در انگلیسی بریتانیایی should + فعل یا خودِ شکل سادهٔ فعل می‌آید.
T: The bare verb doesn't change: It's vital that she attend (not attends). We insist that he be informed. | شکل ساده تغییر نمی‌کند: It's vital that she attend (نه attends). We insist that he be informed.
T: In everyday British English "should" is common: The doctor recommended that I should rest. | در انگلیسی روزمرهٔ بریتانیایی should رایج است: The doctor recommended that I should rest.
E: The landlord insists that the rent be paid on the first of the month. | صاحبخانه اصرار دارد اجاره اول ماه پرداخت شود.
E: It's essential that every visitor sign in at reception. | لازم است هر بازدیدکننده در پذیرش ثبت‌نام کند.
E: They suggested that we should apply online. | پیشنهاد کردند آنلاین درخواست بدهیم.
P: gap | It is vital that he ___ the form before Friday. | submit ; submits ; submitted
P: gap | The council demanded that the rubbish ___ removed. | be ; is ; was being
P: gap | I recommend that she ___ see a specialist. | should ; would ; must to
P: choose | Which is formal and correct? | We request that all phones be switched off. ; We request that all phones are switching off. ; We request all phones be switching off.
P: order | It's important that everyone arrive on time.
P: transform | Use "should": "The manager insisted that we stay late." (…that we should…) | The manager insisted that we should stay late.
M: gap | She insisted that her son ___ home by ten. | be ; is ; was being
M: gap | It's essential that the patient ___ nothing before surgery. | eat ; eats ; ate
M: choose | Which is correct after "It's essential that…"? | he be told ; he is telling ; he to be told
M: order | They proposed that the meeting be postponed.
M: transform | Use the bare verb: "It's vital that she should arrive early." (It's vital that she…) | It's vital that she arrive early.

# c1_ellipsis | C1 | Ellipsis and substitution: I hope so, so do I, do so | حذف و جایگزینی: I hope so، so do I، do so | R: Agreeing and disagreeing
T: To avoid repeating words, use "so" and "not" after think, hope, expect, suppose, I'm afraid: "Is it open?" "I think so." "I hope not." | برای پرهیز از تکرار، بعد از think، hope، expect، suppose و I'm afraid از so و not استفاده کنید: I think so. I hope not.
T: Agree with "So do I / So am I" and "Neither do I / Nor can I". The auxiliary matches the first sentence. | برای موافقت So do I / So am I و Neither do I / Nor can I؛ فعل کمکی با جملهٔ اول هماهنگ است.
T: "Do so" and "one/ones" replace a verb phrase or a noun: He promised to call, and he did so. I'd like the red one. | do so و one/ones جای گروه فعلی یا اسم می‌نشینند: he did so. I'd like the red one.
E: "Will it rain tomorrow?" "I'm afraid so." | «فردا باران می‌آید؟» «متأسفانه بله.»
E: "I can't stand queues." "Neither can I." | «از صف متنفرم.» «من هم همین‌طور.»
E: If you haven't registered yet, please do so today. | اگر هنوز ثبت‌نام نکرده‌اید، لطفاً امروز این کار را بکنید.

P: gap | "Is the shop still open?" "I don't think ___." | so ; it ; not
P: choose | Which reply is correct? "I've never been to Wales." | Neither have I. ; Neither I have. ; So have I never.
P: order | I hope not, to be honest.
P: transform | Avoid repeating: "I'd like the blue shirt, not the white shirt." (…not the white…) | I'd like the blue shirt, not the white one.
M: gap | "Will we be late?" "I hope ___." | not ; no ; don't
M: gap | "I was really tired." "So ___ I." | was ; did ; am
M: choose | Which replaces a verb phrase? | do so ; so do ; one
M: order | Neither of us can drive.

# c1_formal | C1 | Formal style: nominalisation and hedging | سبک رسمی: اسم‌سازی و احتیاط در بیان | R: formal|letter|would|appear
T: Formal writing often turns verbs into nouns (nominalisation): "We decided to close the branch" becomes "The decision was made to close the branch." | نوشتار رسمی اغلب فعل را به اسم تبدیل می‌کند: The decision was made to close the branch.
T: Hedging makes claims softer and more careful: It would appear that…, This tends to…, It seems likely that…, There is some evidence that… | احتیاط در بیان ادعا را نرم‌تر می‌کند: It would appear that…، This tends to…، It seems likely that…
T: Use these in reports, complaints and work emails, not in a chat with friends. | این‌ها را در گزارش، شکایت و ایمیل کاری به کار ببرید، نه در گپ دوستانه.
E: The introduction of the new system has reduced waiting times. | راه‌اندازی سیستم جدید زمان انتظار را کاهش داده است.
E: It would appear that the payment has not been received. | به نظر می‌رسد پرداخت دریافت نشده است.
E: Young drivers tend to have more accidents. | رانندگان جوان معمولاً تصادف بیشتری دارند.

P: choose | Which is more formal? | There has been a significant increase in complaints. ; Loads more people are moaning. ; People complain much more now, yeah.
P: order | It seems likely that the project will be delayed.
P: transform | Nominalise it: "They arrived late, which caused problems." (Their late…) | Their late arrival caused problems.

M: gap | There is some evidence ___ the scheme is working. | that ; what ; which it

# c1_emphasis | C1 | Emphasis: do, auxiliaries and fronting | تأکید: do، فعل کمکی و جابه‌جایی | R: do|really|emphasis|What
T: Add do/does/did before the main verb to emphasise or contrast: I do like your hair! He did call — I just missed it. | برای تأکید یا تضاد do/does/did را قبل از فعل اصلی بگذارید: I do like your hair! He did call.
T: Stress the auxiliary in speech to correct someone: "You haven't paid." "I HAVE paid!" | در گفتار برای اصلاح حرف دیگری روی فعل کمکی تأکید کنید: I HAVE paid!
T: Fronting puts information first for focus: That film I'll never forget. Strange as it may seem, I enjoyed it. | جابه‌جایی اطلاعات را برای تأکید اول جمله می‌آورد: Strange as it may seem, I enjoyed it.

E: She did warn us about the traffic. | او واقعاً دربارهٔ ترافیک هشدار داده بود.
