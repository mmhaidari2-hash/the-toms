# a2_past_reg | A2 | Past simple: regular verbs (-ed) | گذشتهٔ ساده: فعل‌های باقاعده (-ed) | R: Past simple|past simple|I spilled
T: We use the past simple for finished actions at a finished time: yesterday, last week, in 2019. | گذشتهٔ ساده برای کارهای تمام‌شده در زمانی تمام‌شده است: yesterday، last week، in 2019.
T: Regular verbs add -ed: walk, walked; play, played. If the verb ends in -e, just add -d: live, lived. | فعل‌های باقاعده -ed می‌گیرند: walked، played. اگر فعل به -e ختم شود فقط -d: lived.
T: Watch the spelling: stop, stopped; study, studied. And the past simple is the same for everyone: I walked, she walked. | به املا دقت کنید: stopped، studied. گذشتهٔ ساده برای همه یکی است: I walked، she walked.
E: Nicola jumped in every puddle. | نیکولا توی هر چاله پرید.
E: Tom cooked the turkey on Christmas Day. | تام روز کریسمس بوقلمون را پخت.
E: We stayed in Brighton last summer. | تابستان گذشته در برایتون ماندیم.

# a2_past_irr | A2 | Past simple: irregular verbs, didn't and Did…? | گذشتهٔ ساده: بی‌قاعده، didn't و Did…? | R: What did you|Past simple|past simple
T: Many common verbs are irregular: go, went; have, had; see, saw; buy, bought; eat, ate; take, took. | خیلی از فعل‌های رایج بی‌قاعده‌اند: went، had، saw، bought، ate، took.
T: For the negative, use "didn't" + the basic verb: I didn't go. Not "I didn't went". | برای منفی didn't + شکل سادهٔ فعل: I didn't go (نه I didn't went).
T: For questions, use "Did" + the basic verb: Did you see it? What did you buy? | برای سؤال Did + شکل سادهٔ فعل: Did you see it? What did you buy?

E: Biscuit ate a little bit of the cake. | بیسکویت یک ذره از کیک را خورد.
E: Did you have a nice weekend? | آخر هفتهٔ خوبی داشتی؟

# a2_simple_cont | A2 | Present simple or continuous? | حال ساده یا استمراری؟ | R: present simple|Present continuous|routines
T: Present simple for habits and facts: Tom drives a bus every day. | حال ساده برای عادت‌ها و واقعیت‌ها: Tom drives a bus every day.
T: Present continuous for now: Today he isn't driving. He's watching football. | حال استمراری برای همین الان: Today he isn't driving. He's watching football.
T: Look for clues: every day, usually, never go with the simple; now, at the moment, today, look! go with the continuous. | به نشانه‌ها نگاه کنید: every day، usually، never با حال ساده؛ now، at the moment، today، look! با استمراری.
E: Leslie usually walks to school, but today she's taking the bus. | لزلی معمولاً پیاده به مدرسه می‌رود، اما امروز با اتوبوس می‌رود.
E: Ryan plays football on Saturdays. | رایان شنبه‌ها فوتبال بازی می‌کند.
E: Be quiet! I'm talking to Grandad. | ساکت! دارم با پدربزرگ حرف می‌زنم.

# a2_compare | A2 | Comparatives and superlatives | صفت تفضیلی و عالی | R: Comparatives|bigger|smaller
T: To compare two things, short adjectives add -er + than: Ryan is taller than Nicola. | برای مقایسهٔ دو چیز، صفت‌های کوتاه -er + than می‌گیرند: Ryan is taller than Nicola.
T: Long adjectives use "more": Brighton is more crowded than our street. For the top of a group, use the -est or the most: the tallest, the most beautiful. | صفت‌های بلند با more می‌آیند: more crowded. برای بالاترین در یک گروه: the tallest، the most beautiful.
T: Watch the spelling and the irregulars: big, bigger; happy, happier; good, better, the best; bad, worse, the worst. | به املا و بی‌قاعده‌ها دقت کنید: bigger، happier، good/better/the best، bad/worse/the worst.

E: These are the best chips in England! | این‌ها بهترین چیپس انگلستان است!
E: Today is colder than yesterday. | امروز از دیروز سردتر است.
